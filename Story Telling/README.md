This directory represents the **Data Integrity & Preprocessing** module of the Cisco Data Science Program. It is designed to showcase the critical engineering phase of the machine learning lifecycle: transforming raw, noisy, or historically fragmented information into model-ready datasets.

The work here bridges the gap between raw data collection and high-stakes predictive modeling, ensuring that the resulting business insights are both mathematically sound and narratively compelling.

## 🛠 The "Data Cleaning" Arsenal

This folder serves as a demonstration of technical maturity in handling "messy" real-world data. Key advanced techniques used across these files include:

* **Time-Series Normalization:** Adjusting frequency metrics to account for the massive growth in global publishing volume over 300 years.
* **Periodicity Detection:** Identifying 17-year vs. 13-year patterns in noisy historical datasets.
* **Cross-Domain Correlation:** Merging disparate datasets (e.g., biological eggshell metrics and word-frequency trends) to validate causal hypotheses.
* **Inverse Correlation Analysis:** Mapping how physical scarcity (fishery crashes) creates linguistic abundance (crisis reporting).
* **Proxy Variable Identification:** Using word-frequency shifts as a proxy for physical technological milestones (e.g., the invention of the automobile).

## 🗺 Professional Navigation

These projects are structured to show a progression in data storytelling and analytical depth:

1. **Exploration:** Start with **Plotting Animal Words** to see the basics of trend identification and hypothesis formation.
2. **Biological Correlation:** Move to **Silent Spring** or **Cicadas Are Coming** to see how linguistic data is validated against hard biological metrics.
3. **Complex Crisis Mapping:** Conclude with **Coral Conundrum** or **Warm Waters Peru** to see how technological and climate shifts create complex, non-linear data patterns.
