## 🛠 The 'Data Cleaning' Arsenal

Across these projects, I demonstrate a robust set of advanced preprocessing and visualization techniques:

* **Opinion Disparity Analysis:** Using categorical coloring and consensus mapping to identify "perception gaps" in data.
* **Complex Data Pivoting:** Reshaping datasets from long to wide formats to enable multi-variable longitudinal comparisons.
* **Contextual Anchoring:** Incorporating "Reference Benchmarks" (e.g., Human Breath-hold records or Room Temperature) to provide immediate scale and relatability.
* **Dynamic Direct-Labeling:** Engineering custom Matplotlib functions to place labels at the terminus of lines, eliminating cluttered legends.
* **Geometric Normalization:** Creating new features like "Footprint" or "SHU Ratios" to compare disparate objects fairly across centuries and categories.
